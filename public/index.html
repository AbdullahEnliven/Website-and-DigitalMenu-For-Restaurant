MEET THE CHEF

Chef Dale Hutchins

Dale learned to cook flipping burgers at his uncle's roadside grill outside Tulsa. He has run our kitchen since the day we opened, and he still grinds the beef and bakes the pies himself every morning.

"A diner should feel like somebody's kitchen. Good food, hot coffee and a seat that is always yours."

OUR AMBIENCE

Warm light, wood and good company

Step inside and you will find rows of glowing pendant bulbs hanging from a warm wood-beam ceiling, a patterned tile floor underfoot and cushioned chairs in earthy tan and soft green. Tall glass windows with trailing greenery fill the room with light by day and a gentle glow at night.

It feels just right for a family dinner, a date night or a long catch-up with friends. Choose a cozy table for two, a table for four or a long table for ten, and we will take care of the rest.
